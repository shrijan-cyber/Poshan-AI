# Supplement evidence and interaction handling

## Status

No external evidence or interaction catalog is installed. The current code does not provide drug or supplement interaction checking. Do not treat an empty catalog as evidence of safety.

## Publication contract

Every displayed claim needs the source URL and source name, retrieval/review date, evidence grade, study type, human/animal designation, population, outcome, limitations, and reviewer status. PMIDs and DOIs must resolve to real records. Avoid copying copyrighted abstracts; show citation metadata and link to the publisher/PubMed record.

Interaction lookup status must be explicit: `known`, `potential`, `insufficient-evidence`, or `not-reviewed`; `no-interaction-identified` may only be used after a documented, scoped review. Severity remains absent unless the cited source supports it. Never advise users to start, stop or change medication; direct questions to a pharmacist/qualified clinician.

## Sources

Use NIH ODS/NCCIH reviewed pages and relevant primary literature as sources for human review. Do not adopt automated interaction scores from Hermetica/iDISK/SuppDB as clinical truth. Clinical review and ongoing review-date policy are prerequisites to publication.
