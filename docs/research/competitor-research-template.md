## Evidence standard

Label each statement as one of:

- **Verified fact:** directly supported by a dated first-party product page, help article, terms, app listing, or observed product behavior. Link the source and quote/paraphrase only what it supports.
- **Community signal:** user-posted experience. Record platform, date, sample context, and that it is anecdotal rather than representative.
- **Interpretation:** researcher explanation of evidence. Keep separate from the source fact.
- **Hypothesis:** unverified product or market assumption to validate with users or further research.

Record inaccessible/paywalled material and missing evidence instead of filling gaps from memory. Do not treat advertising language as independently validated outcomes.
